# 35. Purchases

Part of [Qutai: how it works and the decisions behind it](../../DESIGN.md) · [All decisions](decisions-index.md) · [History](history.md)

Buying Qutai Credits in the official (pro) builds: packs sold as one-time ("consumable") in-app products on the App
Store and Google Play, checked through RevenueCat (a payments service that talks to both stores) before any credit is
added. Since 20 Nov 2026 Google Play is connected in the app through RevenueCat's SDK (pro builds with the key only);
the App Store comes later through the same code. A production switch lets the owner's own store test purchases add
credits. The temporary "fake purchases" mode (17–21 Nov 2026) was removed on 21 Nov 2026 (`BUY-21`).

## How it works

1. **Buy credits** opens from setup's account screen, Settings › Account › Qutai Credits and the "not enough credits"
   message. It shows the balance, what credits are, the packs the server offers for this phone's store with **the
   store's own price**, a store note, Terms and Privacy, and **Check for missing credits**.
2. The flow: the payments library is told the Qutai account id before anything is bought; after a purchase the
   store's transaction id is saved on the phone first, then reported to the server; the server asks RevenueCat itself
   and only then adds the pack's credits, and the new balance is shown from the server's answer. If the report gets
   lost, RevenueCat's notification to the server credits it anyway, and the phone reports saved purchases again when
   Buy credits opens, on "Check for missing credits" and after sign-in; a purchase the phone never got to save (killed
   app, no transaction id, "already owned") is found in the store's list of recent purchases and reported then.
3. A refund at the store takes the credits back; the balance may go below zero, which stops spending until the user
   buys again.
4. Which store: RevenueCat in a pro build with RevenueCat's key for the platform (Android today), its native module in
   the build and a signed-in account; otherwise an "unavailable" store: Buy credits shows the balance and packs
   and says buying isn't available in this build (every iOS build today, a pro build without the key, Expo Go).
5. There are no fake purchases any more (removed 21 Nov 2026, `BUY-21`): no "Test mode" notice and no **Get (test)**
   button on any server; every Buy button is a real store purchase at the store's price.
6. There is no "Restore purchases" button: one-time products can't be restored at the stores and credits live in
   Qutai's ledger; "Check for missing credits" is the recovery path.
7. Test purchases in production (owner, 20 Nov 2026): panel › Settings › Test purchases in production lets store test
   purchases add credits on the production server, only for the accounts listed there and only while it is on.

Merging can bring back a purchase that was just dropped; it is only reported once more, which is safe (`BUY-28`). The list of answered purchases is likewise not written over when it couldn't be read.
  Status: Default (implementing session, 21 Nov 2026 code review; open to change) · Why: before, a failed read counted as an empty list and that empty list was saved, which could delete every purchase still waiting for its credits · Enforced: src/purchases/flow.ts (edit, withUnsaved, settle), src/purchases/index.ts (onStorageFailure) · Tests: scripts/credits-tests/purchases.test.ts

- **`BUY-36`** "Check for missing credits" (and opening the Buy credits screen) asks the server about every kept purchase at the same time, once each, without the Retry-After waits a fresh purchase gets; one not found yet stays kept for the next check. The screen no longer shows how many purchases are waiting; a check that finds some still waiting says the payment went through and the credits come shortly.
  Status: Decided by the owner (24 Nov 2026: the count was confusing and the check hung) · Why: one at a time with three tries and waits of up to 15 s, ten kept purchases kept the check busy for minutes · Enforced: In the app: src/purchases/flow.ts (reportAll, report tries), src/components/BuyCredits.tsx (lateStatus); scripts/credits-tests/purchases.test.ts

- **`BUY-37`** How many credits a pack gives is changed in the admin panel at any time (Prices and packs › a pack's **Credits…**; audited with the admin's Access email, the time, a reason, and the amounts before and after): a whole number from 1 to 1,000,000 credits, applied to every store's row of that product id so one product gives the same amount in both stores; retired packs can be changed too. The new amount applies to every purchase the server records after the save, including one bought earlier that reaches the server only now; a purchase already recorded (credited, held, or refunded first) keeps the amount it was recorded with, and a refund takes back what was given. The store price, the product id and the order are not changed by it. A deploy never writes packs (a prices file that names `credit_packs` stops the deploy), so an edited amount survives. Phones show the new amount within about two minutes (`/config` is cached 60 s by HTTP and 60 s in the app); the app shows what `/config` gives, so no app change.
  Status: Decided by the owner (25 Nov 2026: tune pack sizes without a redeploy) · Why: pack sizes are an open business decision (backend/DESIGN.md › Decisions) and a new store product for every change is slow · Enforced: Server: backend/apps/admin/worker/api/actions.ts (setPackCredits); backend/apps/api/src/purchases/service.ts (applyPurchase records the amount once); the production deploy script's priceFiles; backend/scripts/check-seed-prices.mjs · Tests: backend/apps/admin/test/actions.test.ts; backend/apps/api/test/purchases.test.ts · Source: backend/README.md › Credit packs

## Details

**In the app**

- (was `BUY-14`) The app saves the store transaction before reporting it, asks again after the server's wait (at most 15 s, three tries), then keeps it and reports it later. Why: "a crash or a lost connection right now can't lose it". Where: In the app: src/purchases/flow.ts (createPurchaseFlow); src/purchases/index.ts (reportPendingPurchases); README.md › Buying credits.
- (was `BUY-20`) The app switched to the fake store because the server's configuration said so, not because of a build setting. Why: no build flag needed. Removed with the fake purchases on 21 Nov 2026 (`BUY-21`): the app no longer reads `purchases.mode`.
- No "Restore purchases" for consumables (checked 20 Nov 2026): Google Play's billing guide asks apps to process purchases on start and resume, not for a restore button; RevenueCat says `restorePurchases` should only follow a user's tap and consumables restore only with custom App User IDs. "Check for missing credits" covers it (`BUY-28`).
- Android `launchMode` stays `singleTask` although RevenueCat says it can cancel a purchase when the user leaves and returns through the launcher icon (nothing is charged then); `singleTop` is the fix if it shows up. Where: README.md › Buying credits › Android launch mode; docs/native-code.md.
