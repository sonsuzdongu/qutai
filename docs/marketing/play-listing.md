## English (en-US)

**App name** (25): `Qutai: Agentic AI Browser`

**Short description** (74): `An AI browser that knows you, fills in forms, shops and researches for you`

**Full description:**

```
Qutai is an agentic AI browser: an open-source web browser whose assistant does the work on the page for you. Tell it what you need in your own words, by typing or by voice, and it works in your tabs step by step while you watch.

WHAT THE AI AGENT DOES FOR YOU
• Gets to know you: tell it your addresses, sizes, diet or budget once, and it uses them in later tasks
• Fills in forms with your details: sign-ups, applications, delivery and contact forms
• Shops for you: finds the product, picks your size, adds it to the cart and enters your delivery details
• Books a table or an appointment, then offers to add it to your calendar or set a reminder
• Researches for you: deep research searches the web, reads page after page and writes a report with its sources
• Reads and compares pages across your tabs, and gives you the result as a table
• Saved tasks you can run again by name, and reminders

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
• Every step is shown as it happens, the page it works on is highlighted, and you can stop it at any time
• It asks before it uses your saved details on a site for the first time
• Payments stay yours: by default it stops before payment and you press the final button; if you let it pay, it asks you every time
• It never types passwords, card numbers or one-time codes
• Per-site permissions: let the assistant act, ask before each action, only read, or stay out
• Calls, texts, WhatsApp messages and emails are prepared as drafts that you send
• "What was sent to the AI" lists every request: time, provider, model, site and content

CHOOSE HOW THE AI RUNS
• Your own API key from Anthropic, OpenAI, Google Gemini or OpenRouter, or your own OpenAI-compatible server (such as Ollama, LM Studio or vLLM). Qutai charges nothing; you pay your provider directly.
• Or sign in and use Qutai Credits, bought in the app, with no key to manage. Credits never expire.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Tabs, history, bookmarks, chats and the assistant's memory are stored on your phone
• Only what a request needs is sent to the AI provider you chose; with Qutai Credits it passes through Qutai's server, which doesn't store or log it
• No analytics, tracking or advertising in the app
• Built-in ad and tracker blocking with the filter lists you choose or your own rules

A COMPLETE BROWSER
• Tab groups, reader mode and read-aloud
• Incognito tabs with an optional biometric lock
• Voice input, QR code scanning, bookmark import and export
• Save pages as PDF or full-page screenshots
• Background playback, sharing to Qutai and a home screen widget
• Encrypted backup to a single password-protected file
• Ten interface languages, including English and Turkish

Qutai is open source under the Apache-2.0 licence: https://github.com/sonsuzdongu/qutai

AI can make mistakes. Check its answers, and review bookings, messages and other important actions before they are completed.

Privacy Policy: https://qutai.sonsuzdongu.com/en/privacy-policy/
```
